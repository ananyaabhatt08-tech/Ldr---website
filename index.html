Questions

Do we both need to buy it?

No. One of you buys and creates the space. Your partner joins with the invite code and pays nothing.

Who can read our journal?

Only the two accounts in your space. Each space is kept separate in the database, and other couples cannot read it.

What if my partner has not joined yet?

You can start right away. Your invite code stays at the top of the page until they join, and everything you wrote is waiting for them.

What do we need to use it?

An email address each and a web browser. Open the page on your phone or computer and sign in.

I bought it. Where is my code?

Your code is shown on your purchase confirmation. If you cannot find it, email the seller.
