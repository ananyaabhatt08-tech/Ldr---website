Making it work

📞 Ritual over routine

Pick a regular call time you both protect, and add small rituals: good-morning texts, a show you watch together.

🗓 Always have a next visit

Having the next reunion on the calendar makes the distance feel temporary.

💬 Share the small stuff

Photos of your lunch, your commute, your day. The ordinary details build closeness.

🤝 Talk about the hard parts

Jealousy, loneliness and bad days are normal. Name them early and kindly.

🎁 Mail something real

Handwritten letters and little care packages hit differently than a text.

🧭 Plan the end of distance

Talk openly about when and how you will close the gap, even if it is rough.
