Our story

It was pouring in Chennai

Two strangers, two umbrellas, and a book that hit the pavement. Here is how it went from there.

  1. July 2019

    Two umbrellas

    A monsoon evening, a crowded street, and two umbrellas that bumped right into each other.

  2. Same evening

    A dropped book

    One book fell. The other person picked it up, and then held an umbrella over both of them.

  3. Still July 2019

    One cup of coffee

    It was meant to be quick. They talked for five hours until the cafe turned the lights down.

  4. 2022

    First trip together

    Two passports, one very long queue, and the first of many arguments about who packs lighter.

  5. 2025

    A question, and a yes

    Under a borrowed umbrella, of course.

  6. Today

    Still going

    Every anniversary, we go back to where it began and stand in the rain on purpose.

Dear you,

I still think about that evening in Chennai. The rain, the book, the way you held the umbrella a little too far over my side and got soaked on yours.

Thank you for the coffee that turned into five hours, the trips, the arguments about packing, and every ordinary Tuesday in between. I would stand in that rain again, every single time, if it meant meeting you.

Happy anniversary. There is a lot more story left to write.

Forever yours
