Our story

It was pouring in Chennai

Two strangers, two umbrellas, and a book that hit the pavement. Here is how it went from there.

  1. July 2019

    Two umbrellas

    A monsoon evening, a crowded street, and two umbrellas that bumped right into each other.

  2. Same evening

    A dropped book

    Priya’s book fell. Arjun picked it up, and then he held his umbrella over both of them.

  3. Still July 2019

    One cup of coffee

    It was meant to be quick. They talked for five hours until the cafe turned the lights down.

  4. 2022

    First trip together

    Their first trip together, on a flight Arjun is still convinced he booked first.

  5. 2025

    He asked, she said yes

    Under a borrowed umbrella, of course. Now we’d love you to be there for the rest.
